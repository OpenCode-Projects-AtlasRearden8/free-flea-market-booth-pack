# Rain Plan Checklist — setup

**Prints:** 1 page, US Letter, portrait.

## What it is

The rain checklist an experienced booth runner does without thinking: pack the
tarps and plugs the night before, move cash and electronics in the first 60
seconds of a shower, and decide go / no-go **before leaving home**. Rain is a
setup question, not a disaster you feel your way through at 6 a.m.

## How to use it

1. **Box 1: pack it rain-ready.** This list goes in the car **even on a clear
   morning**. Two tarps, four weights, a stack of plastic tubs, one zip-bagged
   spare shirt, and a towel? That box turns a shower from a bad memory into
   an anecdote.
2. **Box 2: when the sky darkens.** Cash bag first (into the car or your lap),
   then electronics and paper stock into a lidded tub, then clothes lifted
   and covered, then tarp down, corners clamped, then tilt the pile so
   water runs toward the back. Signs come off the table edge last.
3. **Never leave the cash unattended** during a rain scramble. The 45-second
   noble run to save a quilt is how a booth loses a day's money.
4. **Box 3: go / no-go is decided at home.** Check the hour-by-hour forecast,
   mark it, and be honest: a sprinkle = go, a named storm = no. The sheet
   gives you three pre-decided flavors: GO (small float, easy-sell stock),
   SHORT DAY, NO-GO with a backup venue.
5. **Write the backup before the season starts.** "If the market is a total
   loss, I will..." — an indoor swap meet, a neighbor market, a rain-date
   car boot, or simply "go home and sell online". Deciding at home is free.

## Pairings

- The rain-plan folder is a line on the **Load-In / Load-Out Sheet** booth
  kit checklist.
- A rain day that still sells feeds the same **Cash Tally Log**.

## Timing

Run the whole sheet the night before, then a 60-second glance of box 2 at
5 a.m. under the actual sky.
