# Big Readable Price Signs — setup

**Prints:** 2 pages, US Letter, portrait. Page 1: $1 / $2 / $5 / $10 table
signs. Page 2: FILL A BAG, MAKE AN OFFER, CASH ONLY.

## What it is

Big-block signs that read across a booth at ten feet, plus the three "policy"
signs (fill-a-bag, offers, cash only). Blank write-in lines let you add a note
such as *"everything red"* or *"make me a bundle deal"*.

## How to use it

1. **Print the pages you need.** You do not have to print both pages — most
   first-time booths only need two table signs and CASH ONLY.
2. **One price per table.** The trick of these signs is honesty: put $1 on the
   table, and every single thing on it is $1. Sort at home.
3. **Tape, do not lay down.** Signs taped to the table edge, a crate, or a
   canopy leg at roughly eye height are the ones shoppers actually read.
   A sign lying flat on a blanket disappears.
4. **Write before you go.** Fill in the small line under each sign at home
   ("tools only", "mismatched mugs") so you are not doing it in the wind.
5. **CASH ONLY is a promise.** Put it up only if you actually mean it — some
   markets do not have card readers worth trusting, and a printed sign stops
   the awkward ask.

## Pairings

- Put prices on the items themselves with **Price Stickers & Tag Strips**.
- Count what the signs sell with the **Cash Tally Log**.

## Timing

Print at home the night before. Bring the full sheet even if you use half —
signs get rained on, taped over, and carried off by the wind.
