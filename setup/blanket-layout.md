# Blanket Layout Map — setup

**Prints:** 1 page, US Letter, portrait.

## What it is

One map of your booth space. You fill in where the blanket goes, where the till
lives, and where each group of items sits — before you leave home, while you can
still think straight.

## How to use it

1. **Print one copy per market visit.** Tape it inside your cash box lid or
   under the tablecloth where only you can see it.
2. **Check the blank boxes first.** The default ZONE A outline assumes a
   blanket; swap the note if you use a table or canopy.
3. **Write your own item groups in.** The printed zones (tools, electronics,
   toys, housewares, clothes, box lot) are suggestions — cross out any that do
   not sell that day and write the real group name in.
4. **Point the price signs at the aisle.** The "AISLE SIDE" strip at the bottom
   of the map is a reminder: big signs belong on the aisle edge, one per
   blanket edge.
5. **Keep the till zone small.** Cash bag, change, tally log, pens fit in one
   shoebox — that is the whole CASH & TILL box.

## Pairings

- Pairs with **Big Readable Price Signs** (the aisle edge) and the
  **Load-In / Load-Out Sheet** (the load-in row for the table, blanket, and
  layout map itself).

## Timing

Fill it out the night before, at the kitchen table, ideally while you are
actually packing the boxes. Ten minutes there saves an hour of re-arranging at
6 a.m. while shoppers walk past.
