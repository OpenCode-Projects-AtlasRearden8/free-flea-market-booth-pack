# Load-In / Load-Out Sheet — setup

**Prints:** 2 pages, US Letter, landscape. Page 1: box tracker. Page 2: booth
kit checklist and the day timeline.

## What it is

The list that ends the most common booth heartbreak: driving home and
remembering the till lid holding the exact pen that sells, or the price signs
still taped to a table you borrowed. Every box gets a row; every phase of the
day gets a checkbox.

## How to use it

1. **Fill the tracker at home.** One row per box or bundle ("kitchen bake
   ware", "framed prints"). Where it is, what it is, what you paid for it if
   you care to know your margins later.
2. **Brought = physically in the car.** Not "on the porch", not "next to my
   keys". Sheet rules: no checkmark before the car trunk closes.
3. **Sold out? Went home? On sale next?** Three tiny columns that turn a
   market    day into next market day: what to re-price, what to re-stack,
   and what to bring back while it still exists.
4. **The booth kit box never varies.** Table, signs, stickers, cash box and
   float, tape, bags, stub pad, water, wipes, rain folder. Check it once at
   4:30 p.m. against the printed list; skip the 6 a.m. "did I pack tape"
   argument with yourself.
5. **Follow the timeline on the right.** 4:30 p.m. boxes; 9 p.m. float
   counted; 5 a.m. park close; 6 a.m. set. Load-out begins at 2 p.m.
   **before** packing: the last hour sells more than it looks like, and a
   booth that is half packed away at 2:45 sells nothing at all.
6. **The two sweeps.** 3 p.m.: booth sweep — sit in the now-empty space and
   look. 3:20 p.m.: car sweep — nothing left on the ground, nothing
   borrowed, nothing forgotten in the booth neighbor's truck.

## Pairings

- Feeds the **Blanket Layout Map** (the layout) and the **Cash Tally Log**
  ("what sold 100%" line, and the day's expenses).
- The "went home" column is where the next market's packing actually starts.

## Timing

Write it once a week before the market; re-check the morning of. Two minutes
per phase; saves you a return trip to the fairgrounds.
