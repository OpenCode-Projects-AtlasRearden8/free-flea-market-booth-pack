RAIN-PLAN CHECKLIST
Rain at a flea market is a setup question, not a mood. Work this list the night before and again at 6 a.m., and the rain becomes an “annoyance” instead of a “disaster”.

PACK IT RAIN-READY (night before)

  • 2 tarps (one over the booth, one under/behind the stock)
  • 4+ bungee cords and 8 clamps (a.k.a. “tarp clips”)
  • Weights: 4 gallon jugs or 4 bricks for canopy legs
  • Plastic tubs with real lids (not boxes) for one shot-grabber
  • Big trash bags — instant sleeves for boxes and bags of stock
  • Old bath sheet or spare blanket as a squeegee
  • Sheet of plastic OR shower curtain for the till
  • Duct tape + scissors + sharpie
  • Spare shirt and jacket in a zip bag for YOU
  • Towel in the car for the drive home

BOOTH MOVES (when the sky darkens)

  • Cash box into money bag, money bag into the car or your lap
  • Electronics and any paper stock into the nearest tub, lid on
  • Clothes lifted off the blanket: one big bag over the rail or rack
  • Tarp spread over stock, corners clamped or bagged-down
  • Tilt stock pile so water runs off toward the back
  • Signs pulled off the wet table edge and put in a bag
  • Sit on the cash — never leave it even for a 60-second run
  • Offer a “rain check” out loud: most shoppers wander off, the bargainers stay

GO / NO-GO — DECIDE IT BEFORE LEAVING HOME

Forecast at 6 a.m.:
Map shows rain in hours — pack heavy, arrive early, still go?
GO — it is only a sprinkle, and the serious sellers show up anyway
SHORT DAY — small float only, easy-sell stock only, skip big-ticket goods
NO-GO — call a market friend and check the indoor venues
