Quick change formulas (lid arithmetic — price minus cash given = change back)
Customer pays $10 for $8 item → $2 back  •  pays $20 for $8 → $12 back  •  pays $5 for $3.50 → $1.50 back
Break a $5:   $1 × 4 + $1 coin  •  Break a $10:   $5 + $5  •  Break a $20:   $10 + $10
Never hand coins into the “what do I owe” hand — say the change out loud, count it into their palm, and only then move on.
